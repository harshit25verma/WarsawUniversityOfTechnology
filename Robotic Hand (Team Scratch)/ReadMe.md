## `/ overview`

> A motor-controlled robotic hand, designed, calculated, modelled, and 3D printed within a 45-day sprint by a 4-person team at the **KV Humanoid Club**, Faculty of Mechatronics, Warsaw University of Technology.

The hand was developed from concept to functional prototype — covering mechanical design, motor selection via MATLAB calculations, 3D modelling, part printing, and movement code.
